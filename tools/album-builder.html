SSCPC Album Builder

Add a new album to the website without coding. Nothing is uploaded anywhere: your photos are prepared on this device.

  1. Fill in the form, choose the photos (any number, 200+ is fine), untick the ones you do not want, and press Prepare album.
  2. Download the ZIP and unzip it into the website folder (it adds a folder inside images/albums).
  3. Copy the album code, open js/data.js, and paste it just after the line const ALBUMS = [.
  4. Upload the website folder to Netlify again.
