Terms and Conditions

  1. Authorised use. The SSCPC admin portal is for members and helpers authorised by the club to manage the club website and its records.
  2. Keep the shared passkey private. Share it only with people the club has approved. Never post it publicly or include it in screenshots.
  3. Your username is personal. One person, one account. Do not create accounts for other people or sign in as someone else.
  4. Responsibility. You are responsible for what is done with your account. Report anything suspicious to a club administrator straight away.
  5. Permissions. New accounts are General Users with limited access. Only administrators can grant more, and they may reduce or remove access at any time.
  6. Your information. We store your name and username to run the portal and manage access. The shared passkey is never stored in readable form. Your details are used only for club administration.
  7. Suspension. Misuse of the portal, its data or other members' information may lead to your account being suspended.
  8. Changes. The club may update these terms or change the shared passkey. You will then need to use the new passkey to sign in.
