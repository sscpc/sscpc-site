Light sweep tab transition
Copy these three files into the repo, keeping the same folders, then commit:
  js/router.js    (rewritten: plays the sweep when a tab link is clicked)
  js/reveal.js    (one line changed: don't replay the old tab under the sweep)
  css/style.css   (new styles added at the very bottom)

Tuning (js/router.js):
  MS = 470            time of each half of the sweep (ms)
  S = H*.45           how much the edge leans
  E = W*.5            how long the screen stays fully covered
Colour comes from --bg and --glow in css/style.css.
