Home hero: tilted photo wall (effect B) behind the hand, orb and text
Copy these files into the repo, keeping the same folders, then commit:
  js/intro-wall.js   (NEW: builds the wall from the photos in js/data.js)
  index.html         (adds the wall layers inside #introStage and the intro-wall.js script tag)
  css/style.css      (new styles added at the very bottom)

Photos: every album cover plus evenly spaced photos from each album (small thumbs where an
album has a thumbs folder). New albums added in the admin portal appear automatically.
Tuning:
  css/style.css   --ws (.55)     how strong the photos are, 0.15 faint to 1 full
  css/style.css   .intro__duo    the blue tint over the photos (opacity .62)
  css/style.css   introWallPan   70s = pan speed; make it bigger for slower
  js/intro-wall.js  WANT (24)    different photos used;  CELLS (70 desktop / 40 phone)  tiles on the wall
The wall does not follow the cursor. It stops animating once the intro has scrolled away,
and is a still image for visitors who prefer reduced motion.
